Northwind Labs Leave Policy

Annual leave: Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave must be requested at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year.

Sick leave: Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required if the absence is longer than 2 consecutive days.

Parental leave: Primary caregivers get 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers get 4 weeks. Parental leave can be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

Notice period: The notice period for resignation is 30 days for employees in their first two years and 60 days after that.

Work from home: Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Core hours are 10 AM to 4 PM.

Public holidays: The company observes 12 public holidays each year. The list is published every January on the HR portal.

Contact: Questions about leave can be sent to people@northwindlabs.example.
